## 1. 職場專業開場句型
- "Good morning, everyone. I'm excited to join the team as the new MIS intern."
- "I'm looking forward to working closely with all of you."
- Small talk 安全話題：天氣、交通、辦公室週邊美食、週末休閒（避免政治、宗教與個人隱私）。
