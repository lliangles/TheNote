# 💼 商英 Unit 04: 商務電話溝通與留言應對 (Telephone Conversations)

> **授課教師**：王淳瑩 老師  
> **核心主題**：專業電話接聽禮節、轉接分機、確認通話訊號、替他人記錄留言 (Taking a Message)

---

## 1. 電話必備實用句庫
- 接聽："Thank you for calling ABC Tech. This is Alex speaking. How may I assist you?"
- 轉接："May I ask who is calling? Please hold the line, I'll transfer your call."
- 留言："I'm afraid Mr. Chen is in a meeting right now. Would you like to leave a message?"
- 確認細節："Could you please spell your last name for me?"
