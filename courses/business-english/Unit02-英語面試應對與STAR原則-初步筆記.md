---

## 1. STAR 結構化回答框架
- **S (Situation)**：設定情境與挑戰背景。
- **T (Task)**：說明自己當時所肩負的核心任務與目標。
- **A (Action)**：具體闡述自己採取了哪些專業行動（強調主動性與團隊合作）。
- **R (Result)**：以**量化數據**呈現最終正面成果。

```text
範例句型：
"In my sophomore year (Situation), our team was tasked with developing an inventory database (Task). 
I took the lead in designing the ER diagram and normalized the tables to 3NF (Action). 
As a result, we reduced query latency by 40% and earned the top grade in class (Result)."
```
