# 💼 商英 Unit 06: 英語簡報技巧與架構表達 (Giving Business Presentations)

> **授課教師**：王淳瑩 老師  
> **核心主題**：簡報三部曲 (開場 Hook、主體過渡語 Signposting、結尾與 Q&A)、解讀圖表數據 (Trends & Figures)

---

## 1. 簡報過渡引導語 (Signposting Language)
- 開場："Today, I'd like to walk you through our new database optimization plan."
- 推進："Moving on to our next point, let's examine the server performance."
- 解讀圖表："As you can see from this bar chart, user traffic peaked in Q3."
- 結尾："To wrap up, I'll now open the floor for any questions."
