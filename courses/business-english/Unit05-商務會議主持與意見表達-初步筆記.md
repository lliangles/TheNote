# 💼 商英 Unit 05: 商務會議主持與意見表達 (Business Meetings)

> **授課教師**：王淳瑩 老師  
> **核心主題**：宣讀會議議程、委婉表達贊成與反對、禮貌打岔 (Interrupting politely)、會議記錄撰寫

---

## 1. 會議討論高階句型
- 表達不同意見："I see your point, but from a technical perspective, I'm concerned about..."
- 委婉打岔："Excuse me for interrupting, but could we clarify the deadline first?"
- 達成共識："It seems we're all in agreement on this proposal."
